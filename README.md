# MarketSense Intelligent Portal

Market Sense Intelligent Portal is a web application designed to help users explore, analyze, and track emerging markets across various sectors and regions. The platform provides detailed insights into market size, growth trends, risk levels, and key players, enabling informed decision-making for business and investment purposes.

Key features include:

Market Explorer: Browse and filter markets by category, region, and growth level.Dynamic Filters and Search: Quickly find markets of interest with search and filter functionality.

Market Details: View in-depth information for each market, including description, metrics, and top players.

Competitor Analysis: Helps analyse companies competing in a market.
